Privacy policy

gmail-mcp is used only by its owner, with his own Gmail accounts.

  • It runs only on the owner's computer; sign-in tokens stay in that computer's keychain.
  • It reads, labels, archives, trashes and drafts mail only at the owner's request. It never sends mail or permanently deletes anything.
  • Mail the owner asks about is shown to the AI assistant he is using for that request. Nothing is sold, shared, or stored on any server.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
